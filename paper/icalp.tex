\begin{abstract}
The Schulze method is a widely used single-winner election method. One alternative beats another when the strongest path from one to the other is stronger than the strongest path in the opposite direction. Since the strength of a path is determined by its weakest link, this can be expressed using the Kleene star of a matrix over the semiring \(\langle \mathbb{Z}^{\pm\infty}, \max, \min\rangle\). Thus, the main properties of the Schulze method follow from the algebraic properties of this semiring. 

\end{abstract}
